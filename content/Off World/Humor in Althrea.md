In creating a world that's meant to be "mature" and full of horrors, I found it completely necessary to incorporate humor where I can. As it turns out, a chapter featuring two idiotic buffoons is a wonderful treat when it follows two [[Chapter 0|chapters]] of innocent children being murdered by [[Draeth|mythical beasts]], and another child bearing witness to the moral corruption of his father. 

What kind of humor goes well with a [gilded](https://www.reddit.com/r/worldbuilding/comments/onxy1b/toals_childfriendly_world_classification_chart/) world? Really, any kind. As long as it serves the story in a way that adds to the experience. I don't really know much about specific types of humor, and usually just default back to whatever it is that I find funny. So, the resulting *flavor* of humor in anything I've worked on is really just my own sense, which I often source back to media I consumed as a child like Tom and Jerry.

The concept of comedy in horror, or the understanding of the commonality between the two, has been around for over a hundred years. You can look into that on your own time, but more recent examples point us to movies like American Psycho or The Cabin in The Woods.

These examples are of a deliberate combination of the two genres, but the concept can show up in different ways too. Authors who create great comedy often end up with excellent horror in their portfolio as well. See Curry Barker or Jordan Peele.
