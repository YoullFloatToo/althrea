---
title: Unwelcome to Althrea
---
Althrea does not invite you in.

Here, you will not find a soft bed for landing. No comfortable confines in which to play, colored with the light of the golden sun and bursting lush with life. It's a world of brutal hostility and death.

However, that doesn't mean Althrea is completely inhospitable. It has its healthy share of magic and whimsy, like its particular [[Humor in Althrea|flavor]] of humor and [[Snapper Frog|exploding frogs]]. The people here *do* find a way to live, and even thrive in this world. Yet, in doing so, they bask in a dangerous air of acceptance, ignorant to the workings at their feet.

Buried somewhere deep inside, a cold horror exists. Weaving itself in between the cracks in the earth, wrapping itself around roots and coiling through sands. And sometimes, influencing the minds of men.

Unwelcome to Althrea.

*─ The Narrator*
