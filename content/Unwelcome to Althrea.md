Althrea does not invite you in.

Here, you will not find a soft bed for landing, or comfortable confines in which to play, colored with the light of the golden sun and bursting lush with life. This is a world of brutal hostility and death.

However, that doesn't mean Althrea is completely inhospitable. It has its healthy share of magic and whimsy, like its particular [[Humor in Althrea|flavor]] of humor, and [[Snapper Frog|exploding frogs]]. The people here *do* find a way to live, and even thrive in this world. Yet, in doing so, they bask in a dangerous air of acceptance, ignorant to the workings at their feet.

Buried somewhere deep inside, a cold horror exists, weaving itself in between the cracks in the earth, wrapping itself around roots and coiling through the sands, and sometimes, plaguing the minds of men.

Unwelcome to Althrea.

*─ The Narrator*
