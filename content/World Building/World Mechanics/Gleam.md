The essence of life is figuratively contained in all aspects of the world: in the waters, in the sands, in the winds and the sounds of rustling leaves. It can be felt through all. When a life essence is destroyed, it is transferred elsewhere, and the being is reincarnated. Perishing to a draeth's blight is seen, not as dying, but as having the life essence stolen. It is an unnatural order of things. A person who's life essence is stolen will never reincarnate, throwing off the energy balance of the world, and casting the victim into a perpetual limbo in the depths of [[The Interstice]].
