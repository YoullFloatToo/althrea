The Faarlen Ocean is like a thick band which surrounds the entirety of the central continents, separating them from [[The Shade]] with an immense distance. The vast majority of travelers attempting to explore the world have never seen [[The Shade]], and those who have, could only barely made it out looming in the distance. They dubbed it [[The Shade]] because of the way it obscured the horizon, seeming at first to be nothing more than a shadow.

The Faarlen ocean grows increasingly turbulent the further out one travels. Near [[The Shade]], there is said to be swells with crests like mountain summits and storms with winds strong enough to lift a galleon from the water. The exaggeration of folklore may be at work, regardless, reaching [[The Shade]] presents a nearly insurmountable challenge, and the nature of its existence remains a mystery to all.
