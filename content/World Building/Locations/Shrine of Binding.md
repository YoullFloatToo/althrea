Description
---
A mountain shrine located at the summit of [[Draeth Peak]].

The dead are taken here by their loved ones in hopes to have their lives restored by the rare [[Binding Draeth]] rumored to have visited this location. The sighting was long ago, and most don't believe the [[Draeth]] ever existed. 

A believer is made with nearly every death, and so the mountain is climbed, and the dad are lain.
