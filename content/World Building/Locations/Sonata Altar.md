[[Quinlan Cultist]], as he is known in the modern era, is the [[Unmoored]] who enlisted Sonata Altar's construction at the peak of his short lived pseudo-religious reign over [[Moonlight Isle]]. Quinlan spent his time manipulating the nearby peoples with his preaching, often inserting himself into various familial relationship dynamics. He won people over in this manner, proving to be someone who could solve any problem, and be trusted with the key to your home. Thus, he created a faction of followers who called themselves *The Enlightened*. It is unknown how Quinlan first made contact with the [[Gods]], but as the [[Unmoored]] are an extremely rare phenomenon, not even he knew that his ability was unique, and that he was leading his followers down a road which lead to no destination. For decades, the people of *The Enlightened* failed at contact with the [[Gods]]. The group eventually thinned and it's members spread throughout the continents.

The people of the lands referred to the self proclaimed *Enlightened* as the [[Lunar Cultists]]. 

Generations later, the Sonata Altar still stands as a relic of the past, and a lesson to the modern youth.
