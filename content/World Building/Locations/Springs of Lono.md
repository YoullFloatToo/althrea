Description
---
Draeths source of power is a pool of life. This is where the life essence they steal is deposited, and where the rare binding draeth can pull from to give life. An in world location called the Springs of Lono is said to be a parallel to the real pool. And the volume of water indicates the amount of life essence in the actual pool. The people wish to never see the Springs of Lono overflow, because they believe it means the threshold has been reached, and an age of disease will follow (after [[Moonfall]]). Its forbidden to bathe in these springs as its seen as defiling the stolen life essence of those trapped in [[The Interstice]].
