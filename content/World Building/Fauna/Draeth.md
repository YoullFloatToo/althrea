Rare beasts from beyond [[The Shade]]  whose draw of breath can tear the [[Gleam]] out of any living being. They have bones made of metal, which can be seen in their teeth. And black shadowy skin, whisps of black smoke trail off of them.

When a draeth decides to use its blighting power, it first exhales. One can hear the deep hiss as its powerful lungs compress, expelling all the air within. This is different than normal breathing patterns, and is a telltale sign of the blight, as the draeth is priming itself to inhale deeply. When the blighting breath finally happens, the draeth inhales vigorously, producing a sharp and painful whistling gust of wind. 

According to [[The Religion]], the following is believed:

When a person's [[Gleam]] is stolen by a draeth's blight ([[Draethblight]]), it is removed from the world. The victim will never undergo [[Reincarnation]], and their mind will become imprisoned in the depths of [[The Interstice]].

A draeth's source of power is a pool of life located somewhere in the expanse of [[The Interstice]]. This is where the life essence they steal is deposited, and where the rare [[Binding Draeth]] can pull from to give life.

This is the extent of knowledge in draethlore.
