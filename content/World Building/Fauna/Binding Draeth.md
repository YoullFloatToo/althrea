A rare breed of [[Draeth]] that possesses the power of life binding, the ability to imbue lifeless beings with vitality. Also known as [[Draethbind]].

This [[Draeth]] is so opposite in nature to any other,  amongst those who believe in its existence, it is considered an entirely different species. Amongst the general populous, it is nothing more than a myth.

Only a single sighting has ever occurred: at the summit of [[Draeth Peak]], now the site of the [[Shrine of Binding]].
