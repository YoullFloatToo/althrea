>"*It's just a frog, and then there's chunks of red meat on your face.*"
>─ someone

A curious frog found in many regions across Althrea. Most known for it's defense mechanism of spontaneous combustion. The method by which it achieves this effect is unknown. However, it is widely presumed to be a defense reaction as it only occurs when approached.

>[!INFO]- Secret
>The existence of this frog is a funny reference to Brandon Sanderson's 2025 Lecture series, during which he used the idea of an exploding frog as an example in explaining the concept of "show don't tell" in writing.
