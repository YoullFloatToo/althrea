The world is encircled with a giant alien barrier of porous black rock rising up out of the ocean. By the people, it is called [[The Shade]].

The [[Faarlen Ocean]] is like a thick band which surrounds the entirety of the central continents, separating them from The Shade with an immense distance. The vast majority of travelers attempting to explore the world have never seen The Shade, and those who have, could only barely made it out looming in the distance. They dubbed it The Shade because of the way it obscured the horizon, seeming at first to be nothing more than a shadow.

The Faarlen ocean grows increasingly turbulent the further out one travels. Near The Shade, there is said to be swells with crests like mountain summits and storms with winds strong enough to lift a galleon from the water. The exaggeration of folklore may be at work, regardless, reaching The Shade presents a nearly insurmountable challenge, and the nature of its existence remains a mystery to all.

Many have ventured out in search of answers and possibly an explanation to the world. They all failed. All but, of course, Captain Juleo and his crew abord The Lady Rosette. As the legend goes, they found a hidden landmass somewhere at the top of the world. A landmass uncharted even on maps rendered by the most prestigious of cartographers. It served as a bridge between The Shade and less treacherous Faarlen waters, allowing them to anchor and travel the remaining distance on foot.

When they finally reached the edge of the world, the alien barrier of porous black rock towering over them, only three remained: Captain Juleo, and two of his most trusted crew. Juleo’s ambition and sacrifices had brought him to that moment and no amount of reasoning would turn him away. Paying no mind to the pleads of his companions, Juleo set foot inside one of the cavities of The Shade and disappeared forever.

The return trip away from The Shade, over the grey landmass and across the Faarlen ocean, is a mystery that toys with myth. Only one of the crew members was abord The Lady Rosette when it crashed upon the rocky shore of northern Vaestra Prime. Although he was able to retell the crew’s launch and arrival at the shade with extraordinary detail, he offered little to recount the events which transpired following the captains exit from this world, and his journey back to the central continents. With  such questionable reliability, and being a lowly seafarer amongst the people, it was decided that he and the rest of the crew had simply gone mad from starvation at sea. His story remains a myth and legend shared amongst children.
