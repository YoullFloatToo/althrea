It is said that when the [[Founder]] created the universe, it took a great mountain of effort. She began to tire under the cumbersome act of creation, so she closed her eyes and lay her head to rest. When she awoke, she found that all her creations had vanished, and that the world had reset. This discovery was a devastating revelation, but she began anew. Again, after an enormous exertion of creation, she closed her eyes and laid her head to rest, only to awake and find that the world had once again been reset.

For millennia, the [[Founder]] created entire universes. Countless forms of life, civilizations, written laws of reality, all collapsed into the void of [[The Interstice]]. She tried again and again, stretching herself thinner each time, but her limit was always reached, and sooner or later she would close her eyes and lay her head to rest, only to wake up and start again.

After creating a universe she was particularly fond of, a vision drew itself in the [[Founder]]'s mind, an epiphany, and she saw the path forward. With one of her hands she reached into her head and pulled one of her eyes out of its socket, planting it into the ground and spawning the first child: The [[Thousand-Eyed God]]. "May he watch over my universe in my slumber, and may I do the same in his." And when she awoke, the [[Founder]] saw her child gazing back upon her with delight, and that her universe had not been undone.

Together, they prevented the world from unraveling, and for centuries it prospered under their gaze. The [[Founder]] anticipated no end to her universe. It had been so long since she last lost her creations that she no longer feared their undoing. Seeing her creations evolve and develop, prospering with such brilliant energy, it filled her with a sense of accomplishment and purpose that fueled her existence.

When she awoke to find her child lying beside her with every one of his eyes closed, lost in a deep slumber, and that the world she cherished so dearly had been wiped from existence, she cried out in pain with a wail so intense that it rattled [[The Interstice]] itself. As the [[Founder]] screamed her sorrows away, an entire universe erupted from her mouth, filling the void with the sounds of music and blinding lights of every hue. And when the [[Founder]] looked down to see her new universe, she did so with tears in her eyes, for what she saw was her most beautiful creation yet.

In the [[Founder]]'s determination to preserve the universe, she was lead to a final act of desperation. She, once again, reached her hands up and plummeted them into her head, ripping her remaining eye out of its socket and planting it into the ground. Another child was spawned, but it wasn't enough. The [[Founder]] recklessly plunged her hands into the back of her mouth and yanked out a tooth. She planted it into the ground, spawning yet another child. One by one, she pulled out all of her teeth, spawning a child each time. She began ripping the hair from her scalp till she went bald, each handful spawning dozens of children. An inferno of rage lit up inside her as she decided her universe would never again be undone.

Every rib was torn from her chest cavity. The entire length of her intestines was pulled out of her abdomen. Every muscle, bone, and organ was uprooted from her body and planted into the ground. And when she was nothing more than a pair of hands, she used one to crack the fingers off the other, thrusting every segment into the ground.

[[The Interstice]] had been filled with the thousands children the [[Founder]] had spawned. She had given up her entire existence, and forevermore, the universe was watched over, never to again be undone.
